Privacy policy

video-publish is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

  • It requests YouTube access only to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the owner's channels.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data about other users is collected, stored, shared or sold.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
